TESTA — REQUIRED REAL EVIDENCE AND CAPTURE CHECKLIST
Prepared 11 October 2026 from the available round-two council reviews and EVIDENCE_INDEX.txt.
Purpose: evidence acquisition, not certification of achievement. No owner, completion status, approval or new measurement is asserted by this checklist. Records not supplied to the review may exist elsewhere; “missing” means absent from the reviewed pack.

USE AND CONFIDENTIALITY
The user can supply permissible system logs, task ledgers, version histories, issue records and approval-workflow exports without supplying emails. Do not collect or package emails, email screenshots, feedback, testimonials, client identifiers, identifying programme/service names, credentials, application URLs, endpoint paths or personal details.
Use stable anonymous aliases for teams, services, scenarios, runs, changes and releases. Preserve links between records through those aliases. Remove names, email addresses, usernames, hostnames, repository addresses and identifying free text. Use role-only approval fields, not invented approvers or signatures. Approval evidence may be a permitted structured workflow export or a reviewed extract of an existing acceptance record; a newly written summary is not an original approval.
Capture dates, units, measurement definitions and denominators where permissible. If exact dates are sensitive, use consistent relative dates/intervals and retain exact provenance privately; disclose the resulting limitation. Do not silently alter measurements. Retain an internal source locator and hash for each permitted extract; hashes identify a file, not the truth of its claims.
An anonymised transcription can preserve otherwise confidential measurements, but label it as derived, identify its source and record the checking method. Do not present recreated charts or synthetic runs as client evidence. Inspect all metadata, filenames, screenshots and embedded links before sharing. Keep raw dataset examples and confidential originals outside the submission pack.

AI CRITERIA
A1 — Measured improvement in testing quality
Required real records: comparable prior/manual and AI-assisted artefacts, their review/evaluation results and downstream quality observations where available.
Capture: anonymous artefact/task ID; task type and complexity; requirement/risk coverage; period; tool/model/prompt version; sample selection and exclusion rules; sample counts; metric definition and denominator; accepted/rejected findings or test cases; independent correctness labels; defects detected/escaped or review rework; reviewer role; matched comparison method and uncertainty. Keep volume and difficulty comparable. Record adverse results, not just successes.
Anonymous alternative: a de-identified evaluation table linked to permissible artefact hashes and structured review-result extracts, without requirement text or reviewer identity.
A08 does not close A1. Effort savings and automation coverage are not quality improvement. Use these same quality records for A4's maintained-quality component.

A2 — Originality of AI approach
Required real records: inspected implementation/design versions and evidence distinguishing the contribution from standard tool use. For any adapted-model claim, obtain training/adaptation provenance and serving evidence before linking it to a client outcome.
Capture: component/version/commit alias; architecture and deterministic-versus-model boundary; implemented versus planned features; alternative/prior approach; specific new contribution; model identifier and artifact hash; base model/adaptation configuration; permitted training-data provenance; serving configuration; timestamped inference run ID; prompt/configuration version; input/output hashes rather than confidential content; evaluation protocol and results.
Anonymous alternative: redacted code/configuration extracts and local serving-log extracts using model IDs and anonymous run IDs. A model name in documentation is not deployment evidence.
If asserting model uplift: freeze a valid independent evaluation set, record label review and provenance, check exact/semantic/source overlap and tuning exposure, and evaluate both models on the same set with the same scoring protocol. Record sample counts, metrics and error analysis. The current 112 candidates need validation; do not retrospectively label them independent or claim a comparison was run.

A3 — Sustained value and adaptability
Required real records: time-series adoption/use and evaluated changes to prompts, models or controls; evidence of actual training participation where numeric attendance is claimed.
Capture: period; anonymous team/user counts and eligibility denominators; tasks/runs completed; version changes and reasons; before/after evaluations; regression/review failures; rollback or corrective action; released-effort trend; prompt/control version history; session dates, duration and anonymous attendance counts. Distinguish enrolled staff, active users and regular participants.
Anonymous alternative: aggregated usage and training logs with counts, date ranges and version IDs; redacted change/evaluation history without employee names. Screenshots of configurable controls prove displayed capability only.

A4 — Operational efficiency and reduced cost/waste with quality maintained
Required real records: original Q2/Q3 benefit ledger or permissible reporting export, underlying task calculations and existing approval-workflow records; bounded timing records for the monthly assurance example.
Capture: quarter and task ID/type; number of tasks; baseline timing source; comparable work volume; AI-assisted execution time; human review/rework time; exceptions; net released effort formula; person-day-to-hour definition; inclusion/exclusion rules; totals of 125 and 132 days; approval date, role, decision and version accepted. For the eight-to-four-hour example capture the single-person monthly task scope, measured months and timing method; mark its inclusion in 257 days.
For cost claims capture licences, infrastructure, implementation, maintenance and review costs with a common period and cost basis; otherwise retain released-effort language. Do not infer that released effort reduced headcount or cash expenditure.
Anonymous alternative: a task-level de-identified ledger and system-generated role-only approval extract. S01 is not that record. Attach A1 quality comparison; human review alone does not prove retained quality.

A5 — Improved stakeholder/customer outcomes
Required real records: measured operational or service-quality outcomes, such as faster assurance decisions, verified accessibility corrections or reduced user-facing failures. No testimonials, feedback or satisfaction messages are requested.
Capture: anonymous workflow/service ID; baseline and follow-up period; outcome metric and denominator; measured decision turnaround or correction completion; linked finding/change/retest IDs; specialist acceptance criterion and result; limitations and attribution to AI versus deterministic checks.
For the 85% activity claim capture eligible activity definition, numerator, denominator, period, exclusions, counting method and separation of deterministic automation from AI assistance. This defines coverage; it does not itself establish improved user outcomes.
Anonymous alternative: aggregate operational logs or a de-identified correction/retest table with hashes and role-only acceptance fields. If no measured outcome exists, disclose that gap rather than construct satisfaction evidence.
