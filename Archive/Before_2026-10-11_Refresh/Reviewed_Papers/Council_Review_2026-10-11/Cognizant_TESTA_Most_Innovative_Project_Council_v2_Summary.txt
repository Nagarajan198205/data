Cognizant Worldwide Limited UK developed a shared regression approach for a UK government client in which service teams maintain their business-critical scenarios and the platform team controls shared execution. The source describes five service teams on the shared platform. The design uses tagged Playwright tests to feed a common regression suite, supported by three Azure DevOps pipelines and a project-generation utility. The approach addresses the central team's difficulty in maintaining every service's domain knowledge. Its contribution is an ownership and execution method designed for reuse, with a triage model intended to direct failures to the responsible team.
