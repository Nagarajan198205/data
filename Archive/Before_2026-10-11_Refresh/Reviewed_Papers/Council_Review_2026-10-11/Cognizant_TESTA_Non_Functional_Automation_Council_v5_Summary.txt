Cognizant Worldwide Limited UK developed PerfOps to automate performance assurance for a UK government client. Pipelines prepare environments, execute workloads, assess results and publish reports. Transcribed visuals show workload volume against target and response-time failures despite passing volume checks. The team reports using a custom JMeter simulation to reproduce abnormal cache growth for remediation testing. Resource checks also exposed missing infrastructure telemetry. Together, these capabilities give engineers a repeatable way to examine service behaviour and the completeness of supporting evidence before advising on release readiness.
