The model serving the local workspace in the exhibits has not been established, and no client run is attributed to it."

## Cross-paper scope conflicts

- **257 person-days** headlines the AI and Innovation summaries. Count it once, in the AI paper, and remove it from the Innovation headline.
- **Code Coverage task (8 to 4 hours)** appears in AI and Innovation. State it once as included in the account total.
- **85% accessibility automation** appears in AI and Innovation. The AI paper owns it. Innovation should describe it as a separate measure.
- **Setup "two to three days" to "minutes"** appears only in Innovation, and conflicts with the evidence boundary that requires either keeping scope or omitting it.
- **Exhibits A01–A08** are cited in AI and Innovation. Innovation's caveat that they are "not records of common client deployment" should be applied in the AI paper too.
- **Model identity:** AI uses "phi4:14b-a11y" and Innovation uses "Phi-4." Align both to "documented design."
- **Team growth (50 to 60 associates)** and co-learning appear in AI and Innovation. Keep one owner.
- **NFT "over 90%"** and AI "257 person-days" measure different populations. Do not present them as one result.
- **Unrelated programme figures** in the new source must not be imported into any paper.
