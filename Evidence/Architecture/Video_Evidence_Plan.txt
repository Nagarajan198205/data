COGNIZANT INTELLIGENT PLATFORM — VIDEO EVIDENCE PLAN
Prepared 11 October 2026. Recording plan only; no video has been recorded or execution result asserted by this file.

PURPOSE
Make the engineering inspectable: show each core engine operating, preserve the finding/evidence trail, and demonstrate the human decisions and change/retest path. Architecture diagrams establish a source-backed design. A recording establishes only the behaviour and environment actually shown; it does not establish historical client savings, general accuracy or production deployment.
Use three short demonstrations, approximately 4–6 minutes each, with a longer separately labelled accessibility improvement case if training or external engineering work requires it. Keep an uncut original privately and provide a permitted edited copy with cuts and elapsed intervals disclosed. Do not manufacture a successful run.

RECORDING CONDITIONS
Use an authorised isolated demonstration environment with synthetic cases and anonymous roles. Do not record the government client's name, emails, feedback/testimonials, email screenshots, credentials, application addresses, tenant/project identifiers or browser chrome showing URLs. Demonstrating the false-positive feature with a synthetic case is permitted workflow evidence; do not expose real confidential user feedback records. Use a cropped app-only view and check notification banners, account menus, logs, ticket links and download names before capture. ADO work items need anonymous text and role aliases; if an approved safe demo board is unavailable, stop at the integration hand-off and record that limitation.
Use app settings to disable LLM calls where supported. Do not revoke shared credentials or change production configuration for the demonstration. Do not run security or load scans against public/third-party targets. Live test targets must be explicitly authorised and controlled.
Show a small evidence slate: solution/version or commit, capture date, local/demo environment, anonymous run/case alias, LLM enabled/disabled, selected model/prompt version where applicable. Keep raw hashes and build/model manifests in the accompanying text evidence. Record the actual selected model; do not relabel a general model as fine-tuned.

VIDEO 1 — INTELLIA11Y CORE AND EXPERT REVIEW
Scene 1: open a synthetic page with a known accessibility case and expected result. Explain what is deliberately present. Show selected scan scope/smart sampling and AI setting.
Scene 2: execute with AI disabled. Open the owned-rule result and its DOM/selector evidence. Show screenshot if capture succeeds; record failure if it does not. This demonstrates the deterministic path, not all WCAG coverage.
Scene 3: execute a comparable AI-enabled scan. Show the real model/prompt identity and distinguish additional interpretation from deterministic findings. Preserve both runs; do not infer correctness merely because counts differ.
Scene 4: as the originating user, flag the designated synthetic finding as a potential false positive. As an expert role, approve or reject using the pending-review path; show separate requested and reviewed status. Trusted direct validation may follow a different path.
Scene 5: with configured demo ADO integration, show the linked story and source target/instructions. Verify creation and screenshot attachment separately. Stop here if the ticket consumer is not available; do not present a story description as completed automated work.
Evidence to retain: aliased input/configuration, two run records, expected labels, screenshot/issue linkage, review transition, work-item creation result and attachment result. No emails or real feedback content.

VIDEO 1B — ONE REAL IMPROVEMENT CYCLE
Choose a case with known expected behaviour. Record the actual person/agent that consumes the story and the reviewed diagnosis. Choose the appropriate change path; it is not necessary to retrain a model for a rule defect.
Rule/prompt path: show a real reviewed diff or prompt-version change, a minimal synthetic HTML regression case with expected labels, baseline failure and corrected pass, plus relevant regression checks. Capture the exact changed version.
RAG path: show an approved anonymous knowledge item, ingestion record and retrieval trace used in a prompt. RAG changes reference context, not model weights. Do not call an embedding update fine-tuning.
Model path, only if justified and available: show approved dataset lineage, independent holdout checks, actual training completion log and adapter hash, conversion/serving manifest, and matched base-versus-adapted evaluation. The inspected builder does not automatically consume the training-session candidate source; verify the actual integration. Resolve the known train/evaluation overlap. Do not train merely to produce award footage without a valid evaluation plan.
After accepted validation, show actual ADO resolution and assignment history. The source story asks for reassignment to the approving person; the requested business process returns to the original submitter. Establish and record the actual routing rather than treating these roles as identical.
As the original user, run a fresh scan against the changed version and verify the specific original case. Confirm the expected outcome or reopen. A bookmarklet showing stored issues is not proof of a fresh scan.
Evidence to retain: issue-to-ticket-to-change-to-test-to-serving-version-to-rescan linkage, timestamps/intervals, expected and observed results, reviewer role, and final acceptance. Record manual hand-offs honestly. A scripted narrative or staged status toggle is not a completed cycle.

VIDEO 2 — INTELLISEC NATIVE DETECTION AND VERIFIED RETEST
Scene 1: select an authorised synthetic repository or controlled local target and an explicit scan mode. Explain native detection versus any optional imported result or advisory feed.
Scene 2: run the native path. Show detector identity/version, evidence and execution status. Demonstrate a meaningful seeded issue with expected behaviour. Do not imply every detector mode ran.
Scene 3: show deterministic processing and, if used, the optional model-verification result separately. Show how incomplete evidence or controls affect the gate, without claiming that a GO verdict is client approval.
Scene 4: review a synthetic finding, retain analyst history, and demonstrate optional ADO linkage if configured. Keep potential false positive and confirmed status distinct.
Scene 5: apply an authorised fix to the controlled fixture, then use the appropriate exact replay or fresh source-detection path. Show fixed, still present or inconclusive honestly. A manually selected fixed status and closed ticket are not sufficient retest evidence.
Evidence to retain: fixture/version, scan mode, detector evidence, gate inputs, review history, controlled change and matched retest. Optional learning rules/suppression changes need separate before/after regression evidence. No universal automatic remediation or model retraining claim.

VIDEO 3 — INTELIPERF NATIVE EXECUTION AND ANALYSIS
Scene 1: open a controlled test plan and show the canonical scenario, load goal and safety limits. If importing JMeter/k6, identify conversion and unsupported constructs; do not call the imported format the executor.
Scene 2: execute a modest authorised workload through InternalRunner. Use safe local diagnostics to show in-process or worker execution, native request counts and timing. Do not use the similarly named simulated PerformanceTestAgent as evidence.
Scene 3: show raw metrics and deterministic derived results, and reconcile at least one calculation. Explain generator health, sample size and run validity. If calibration falls back to an estimate, show that status.
Scene 4: show optional AI interpretation alongside the same recorded measurements. Preserve the fact that collection and core scoring run without a required LLM call.
Scene 5: make a controlled manual plan/service change and rerun under equivalent conditions. Present comparison limitations. Review CI score semantics before interpreting its threshold as a quality decision.
Evidence to retain: plan/version, actual runner identity, safety settings, raw and normalised metrics, calibration status and matched rerun. Evidence signing can be shown only if a real manifest/signature is produced and verified.
Do not demonstrate AutoRemediationService simulation or mock multi-region execution as a live operational fix. No PerfOps/Perf Insights client savings transfer to this application.

ACCEPTANCE FOR THE VIDEO PACK
Every scene maps to a real artifact alias and version. The viewer can distinguish deterministic logic, optional inference, source-present mechanisms, manual work and prototypes. Sensitive content is absent from every frame and metadata. Failures and cuts are disclosed. Each claimed closed loop has linked records, not just a sequence of screens.
Suggested recording filenames: VID-A11Y-01_core-review, VID-A11Y-02_improvement-cycle, VID-SEC-01_detection-retest, VID-PERF-01_execution-comparison. These identifiers are reserved; no files are claimed to exist.

WHAT VIDEO WILL NOT CLOSE ALONE
Historical 257-day/90% savings, 85% activity coverage, release acceptance and client outcomes still require their original defined records. Competitive evidence combines a convincing demonstration with traceable measurements; it does not replace one with the other.
