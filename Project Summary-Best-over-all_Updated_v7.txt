Project Summary

Since July 2022, we at Cognizant have developed automated testing for UK public sector services. Regression automation across Portal, Dynamics and end-to-end testing reached 97.69 per cent. IPAFFS and Risk Engine checks take 0.5 days, down from 4 days. On Salesforce, five service teams maintain the tests for their own services. Across the reported defect population, 3,196 defects were caught before production and two in production. Our 62 colleagues support services handling export certification, import controls and air quality information. In 2026, they gained 36 external certifications.

1. Testing services people depend on

We began this Cognizant engagement in July 2022 supporting a client in UK government. Exporters need usable certificates, importers need dependable border checks and the public needs accurate air quality information. Testing delays can hold up releases. Escaped defects can disrupt those services.

Manual regression for the Import of Products, Animals, Food and Feed System (IPAFFS) and its Risk Engine occupied approximately 4 days per cycle. On Salesforce, changes from one service could affect another. We addressed these problems through automation and shared responsibility for regression, alongside broader functional and non-functional testing.

2. Scope and goals

Our work spans Trade, Common Platform, Operations Proving, Air Quality, the Plant Health Export Service, the Northern Ireland Retail Movement Scheme (NIRMS) and Salesforce Single Customer View. Trade comprises 20 services. We report requirements-to-test traceability for over 10,000 requirements.

- IPAFFS and Risk Engine regression fell from approximately 4 days to 0.5 days per cycle. A numerical target was not supplied (Exhibit 4).
- Trade regression automation reached 97.69 per cent across Portal, Dynamics and end-to-end testing by September 2026. The original target remains unconfirmed (Exhibit 1).
- Five Salesforce service teams contribute tests to the shared regression model, as set out in the platform test strategy.
- Posit upgrade testing is 40 per cent automated and 60 per cent manual, against a target of 90 per cent automated and 10 per cent manual (Exhibit 7).

3. Public value and the longer view

Export Health Certificates processed through the service number over 1.8 million, across 1,552 digital journeys. Exports valued at £657 million have been supported by the Plant Health Export Service. NIRMS reports capacity savings equivalent to 19.5 full-time posts. These are service outcomes to which testing contributes. They are not financial returns attributable to our testing alone.

For Salesforce Single Customer View, we placed test design and maintenance with the five service teams that understand their own workflows. The platform team manages execution and governance. This gives new services a repeatable way to join regression without requiring the platform testers to recreate every team's business knowledge.

At Air Quality, simulated external services let testers exercise over 100 business scenarios when third-party systems were unavailable. In 2026, the client formally recognised this change in testing practice (Exhibit 3). Our wider improvement programme contains 33 initiatives, some operational and others still being implemented (Exhibit 8).

4. Measures we use

Execution and preparation measures show where time was released:

- Trade regression automation stood at 97.69 per cent in September 2026. The historical baseline remains unresolved (Exhibit 1).
- IPAFFS and Risk Engine regression takes approximately 0.5 days per cycle, down from 4 days, shortening release checks (Exhibit 4).
- Risk Engine API regression fell from 36 hours to 5 minutes per release cycle, giving earlier feedback in 2026 (Exhibit 4).
- Each IPAFFS PDF comparison takes 2 minutes, previously 10, reducing document-checking time (Exhibit 4).
- Suspension test data preparation fell from 48 to 4 person-hours per cycle. At twice monthly, this releases 88 person-hours a month, subject to confirmation of the preparation records.

Quality and capability measures cover the reported defect population, upgrade work and team learning:

- Since 2022, pre-production detection accounts for 99.94 per cent of recorded valid defects, showing where problems were found (Exhibit 2).
- Production leakage is 0.06 per cent over the same period. It measures the share of recorded valid defects found after release (Exhibit 2).
- Posit upgrade checks, previously manual, are 40 per cent automated against the 90 per cent target. The remaining work is in progress (Exhibit 7).
- April 2026 client feedback recorded overall satisfaction of 5/5 and a recommendation rating of 10/10 (Exhibit 5).
- Colleagues gained 36 external certifications during 2026. Earlier annual counts have not been supplied for comparison (Exhibit 9).

Apart from Posit, numerical KPI targets were not supplied. Comparable historical defect and satisfaction baselines are also unavailable.

The defect figures cover 3,196 valid defects recorded before production, plus two found after release, since 2022, a total of 3,198. Pre-production detection is 3,196 / 3,198 x 100; leakage is 2 / 3,198 x 100. These measure the reported defect population, rather than automation accuracy. The survey's 10/10 figure is retained as a recommendation rating pending confirmation of its scoring method.

5. How we test

Agile delivery is used across the programmes apart from Operations Proving, where staged validation supports fixed-scope regulatory releases. Testing includes integration and business acceptance as well as accessibility, security and performance. BrowserStack supports checks on Android and iOS.

The tool choices follow the test layer. We use RestSharp and Postman for application programming interface (API) checks, and WebdriverIO with Allure for Air Quality. The Microsoft 365 applications have moved from Selenium to Playwright. Migration elsewhere continues, with completion targeted for the end of 2026. Reqnroll and NUnit replaced the earlier SpecFlow-based setup for end-to-end tests.

For IPAFFS, testers and domain specialists reviewed the automated workflows for the four Common Health Entry Document (CHED) types before scripts were merged. PDF checks compare document contents with the values entered in the application. That comparison tests a user-facing output as well as the underlying transaction (Exhibit 4).

Artificial intelligence (AI) assists test design and scripting. In Air Quality, GitHub Copilot was used across more than 120 regression cases, with a reported 50 per cent reduction in scripting effort. A separate generator uses acceptance criteria and application knowledge to prepare test cases for Azure DevOps import. It checks the output format. Format validation alone does not establish the correctness of a test (Exhibits 3 and 7).

6. Release decisions and stakeholder involvement

The programme reports 20 production releases in 2026. Its delivery account records testing against agreed schedules, although consolidated milestone and budget evidence remains to be supplied. For Packing List Parser (PLP), the initial go-live was in August 2024 and the latest was on 6 August 2026. The team reports no critical production issues or rollbacks over that service history.

PLP migration testing checked transferred data and documents, revised integrations and the public-facing address. Cutover included smoke tests and production validation. Quality assurance sign-off followed the planned checks, and the migration met its agreed date (Exhibit 4).

On Salesforce, release bookings previously relied on a shared Excel file. We replaced it with Microsoft Lists and Power Automate so teams can see available slots, receive notifications and review the change history. Five service teams and client management saw the demonstration. Feedback was positive, with interest in wider adoption.

After 7 months of delivery on Risk Engine and Cloning archival, the client praised the work and recognised the contribution of testing (Exhibit 8). Cognizant's internal Delivery Excellence Team validates the improvement figures, while individual delivery teams approve ideas and implementation. This does not substitute for a programme cost reconciliation.

7. Where testing was difficult

Cross-service regression was the first problem on Salesforce. Each team now applies the @platform-regression tag to its important Playwright cases. The shared pack selects those tests, while failures are routed to the relevant service or platform owner. Five teams already contribute. Their existing tests serve both local and platform checks.

Execution also depended on individual machines and manual starts. We introduced three Azure DevOps pipelines for change validation, on-demand team testing and shared regression. Reports are published after failed runs as well as successful ones. Traces and screenshots help the responsible team investigate.

New teams previously spent 2 to 3 days copying and adapting project files. A utility written in Node.js creates the repository structure and configuration in minutes. The elapsed-time improvement is reported separately from labour savings because the available figures do not reconcile those measures.

Air Quality testing stalled when external systems were unavailable. Virtual services removed that dependency for the covered scenarios and saved approximately 180 hours of effort. Manual PDF comparison presented a different burden in IPAFFS: each automated check takes 2 minutes, compared with 10 previously (Exhibits 3 and 4).

Suspension test data preparation fell from 6 person-days, or 48 hours, to 0.5 person-days, or 4 hours, per cycle. The process runs twice monthly. That releases 44 person-hours per cycle, equivalent to 88 per month at the stated frequency. It is capacity released, not a cash saving.

Repeated application-registration certificate expiries exposed another problem: unavailable test environments. The environment monitor displays each service's expiry status and sends three notifications to a Microsoft Teams channel. Implementation is complete, but the client demonstration is pending with go-live planned before the end of 2026. Its proposed 360-hour benefit remains unverified and is excluded from realised results (Exhibit 12).

10. ROI Summary

The supplied 2026 results demonstrate capacity gains, but do not establish a financial return.

- Total investment since 2022 is not established: people, infrastructure, training and running costs have not been itemised.
- Direct savings claimed for 2026 remain unverified because currencies conflict and savings are combined with revenue (Exhibits 3, 8 and 10).
- Risk mitigation has not been valued. No supported avoided-cost calculation or measurement period was supplied.
- Suspension data preparation releases 44 person-hours per cycle, calculated as 48 minus 4. The reported 2026 frequency is twice monthly, but an hourly rate and supporting records remain outstanding.
- Net benefit, ROI and payback cannot be calculated without total investment and eligible, dated financial benefits. The formula is ROI % = (total benefit - total cost) / total cost x 100.

Internal validation is attributed to the Delivery Excellence Team. No monetary benefits are totalled here. The unverified monitor forecast is excluded. Non-financial gains include regression falling from approximately 4 days to 0.5 days and PDF checks taking 2 minutes compared with 10 previously (Exhibit 4).

11. Why This Merits the Award

Cognizant's public sector testing programme reached 97.69 per cent Trade regression automation and reduced IPAFFS and Risk Engine regression from approximately 4 days to 0.5 days (Exhibits 1 and 4). Those results support services used by exporters and importers.

Five Salesforce teams retain ownership of tests reused across the shared platform. Air Quality's virtual services resolved a recurring testing dependency, while PLP testing supported its scheduled migration (Exhibits 3 and 4). Defect reporting makes release quality reviewable (Exhibit 2). The team earned 36 certifications, offered learning across roles and contributed to school outreach (Exhibit 9). Posit automation and the environment monitor remain unfinished. The delivered results provide a practical basis for continued improvement.
