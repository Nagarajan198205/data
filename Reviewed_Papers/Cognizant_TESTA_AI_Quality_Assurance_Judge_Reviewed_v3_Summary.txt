Cognizant Worldwide Limited UK developed Cognizant Intelligent Platform for quality engineering work for a UK government client. The team reports 85% accessibility automation. IntelliA11y combines repeatable checks with contextual analysis and remediation guidance; IntelliSec supports security investigation; Inteliperf explains performance evidence. Domain-specific model development, retrieval-supported guidance and configurable governance support the approach. Versioned prompts and validation facilities help teams manage change. AI provides specialists with interpretations and proposed actions that they can examine, while engineers retain responsibility for verifying findings and making quality decisions.
