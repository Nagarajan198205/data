Cognizant Worldwide Limited UK embedded non-functional test automation into delivery for a UK government client. Its PerfOps framework automates environment scaling, performance and infrastructure validation, reporting and alerts. The delivery team reports over 90% less manual effort in performance validation and infrastructure validation. Production-informed workloads, Kubernetes testing and six execution profiles support realistic assurance. Dashboards expose release risks, while Perf Insights checks the completeness of test evidence. The work supported releases and migration decisions, including a custom simulation that reproduced bot-driven cache exhaustion and enabled remediation testing.
