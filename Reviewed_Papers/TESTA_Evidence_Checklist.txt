[ ] NFT-01 — Substantiate 90% effort savings
Provide manual hours and automated-process hours for the same scope, including human review and exception handling. Record the number of runs, measurement period, task boundaries and calculation basis. Calculate performance validation and infrastructure validation separately.
Calculation: (manual effort - automated-process effort) / manual effort x 100.
Distinguish measured effort from estimates and elapsed pipeline time.
