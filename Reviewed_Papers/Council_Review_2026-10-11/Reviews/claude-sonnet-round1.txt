3. §4: "Our delivery team reports these benefits through Cognizant's Pulse portal, with review by Delivery Excellence and the engagement delivery lead." This implies a verified approval chain that has not been supplied.
   - *Replace:* "The delivery team reports 125 and 132 person-days net of review time. The portal export and approval record are being compiled and have not been independently inspected."

**Is 4/5 on every criterion defensible? No.** A realistic ceiling today is 1–2 per criterion, perhaps 3 on A4 if the export arrives.

## Cross-paper scope conflicts
- **The 257 person-days** appear in the AI and Innovation papers. Give them one home, in the AI paper only.
- **The coverage task (8→4 hours)** and **the 85% figure** are duplicated across papers. Keep each in the AI paper only.
- **The 50→60 associates**, **the A08 check (59 tests)** and **the impact analyser** also appear in both the AI and Innovation papers. Keep them in one paper only.
- **The NFT paper** closes with "alongside wider accessibility automation" and cites AI-assisted dashboard work. Remove the first. Mark the second as a tooling aid, not an AI result.
- **Savings, shared regression and the specialist platform** are different populations. State a common boundary, or declare the papers as separate entries.
- **Third-party identity** must be supplied separately under organiser rules, with the anonymous client text unchanged. Anonymity is not an exemption.
- **Reconciling counts:** per-paper totals should not sum to a combined claim.

## What editing can and cannot fix
Editing can fix the scope blending, the overreaching phrasing, the summary content and the duplicate claims. It cannot create the portal export, the before/after hours, the release records, the deployed-model proof, the stakeholder evidence or the run records. Until those exist, expect scores of roughly 8, 12 and 8 out of 25.
